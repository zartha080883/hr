          Month summary

          StaffDaysLunch / breaksTotal worked hoursRegularOvertimeMissing sign-outs

          Worked hours = time between sign-in and sign-out minus lunch / breaks (unpaid). Overtime counts worked hours over each person's weekly limit (44 h by default — Ontario's standard; change it per employee). Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

          Payroll

          EmployeeHoursRateOther earningsGrossCPPEIIncome taxOther deductionsNetStatus

          Hours come from the time clock; overtime is paid at 1.5× for hours over each employee's weekly limit. Salaried staff get a twelfth of their annual salary. Enter CPP, EI and income tax from your payroll provider — the desk doesn't calculate statutory deductions. Paid months are locked.
